How do ideas come to one’s head?
Somehow, I started drawing a little island.
It’s tropical and cozy, relaxing and fun.

It works both as a retreat for the mind to rest and for a monkey inside us to play.

Treasure Island↗ by Robert Louis Stevenson subliminally participated in my ideating process.

Now, I’m not sure I could make this thing work in 3D space. It might be a bit too ambitious, so let’s play it safe and start with a proof of concept.

Very first Unity test☝️. Blocked shapes, simple palettes, minimalism — a natural choice for an optimized experience. Previously explored art direction ideas↗ came at help. Also, check this↗.

It felt promising — yet not quite like it. The island felt too small to run around; red shades too harsh; and what would people do in it? Let’s dive deeper.
